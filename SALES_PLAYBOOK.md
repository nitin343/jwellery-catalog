## 🌟 Key Features That Sell This to Local Shopkeepers

### 1. 🧮 Live Gold & Bullion Calculator
- Shopkeepers spend half their day answering: *"Bhaiya, aaj ka sone ka bhav kya hai aur ye kitne ka padega?"*
- Customers can tap **Rate Calculator** at the top or on mobile bottom nav, choose Karat (22K, 24K, 18K), weight in grams, making charges, and 3% GST.
- **WhatsApp Hook:** A button reads *"Lock Estimate on WhatsApp"* which sends the exact breakdown directly to the owner's WhatsApp!

### 2. 📲 Direct WhatsApp Ordering (No Payment Gateway Hassles)
- Local shops don't want payment gateways (avoids 2-3% transaction fees & GST paperwork).
- Every card has an **"Inquire on WhatsApp"** button pre-filled with the title, SKU, price, and image.
- **Wishlist Batch Inquiry:** Customers can save 4-5 items and tap *"Inquire All on WhatsApp"* to send a complete wishlist quotation request in 1 click.

### 3. ✨ 3D Interactive Shimmer & Velvet Aesthetics
- Real-time animated canvas with floating diamond sparkles and gold dust reacting to mouse/touch.
- Looks like a multi-million-dollar Cartier or Tiffany showroom on smartphones.

### 4. 📦 Full Catalog CRUD with Image Upload
- Add new items in 30 seconds.
- Choose between studio presets or upload photos directly from a phone or computer camera.

### 5. 💾 Portable Client Export / Import (Zero Database Setup Needed!)
- **Download Store Data (JSON):** Backs up client settings & products into a single lightweight file.
- **Upload Client Pack (JSON):** Deploy a new client in under 60 seconds by uploading their preset JSON file.
